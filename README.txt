CEDAR LEDGER — QUICK START | HARBORNESTDIGITAL

1. Extract the ZIP and keep every file together. Open index.html in a current Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox desktop browser.
2. Add Customers and Vendors first. Add Inventory products and opening quantities. Create an invoice for a customer; choose a product or leave Product blank for a service. Add payments separately. Purchase orders marked Received add stock. Paid flags affect the cash view.
3. Add expenses, payroll logs, monthly category budgets and manual tax records. Review Overview, Budgets and Reports. Reports show invoice-based operating profit, payments-based cash movement and a simplified balance snapshot. They are not a formal accounting ledger or tax filing.
4. Settings offers five light themes, fonts, spacing, card style, high contrast, currency display and English, Spanish, French or German navigation/key labels. Detailed guidance and your entered records do not auto-translate. Currency changes symbols, not exchange rates.
5. All records save in this browser profile on this device. There is no automatic device sync or account. Export JSON backups regularly; import to transfer between devices. Import replaces that browser's records. Clearing browser data can erase records.
6. Extracted desktop files work offline. Phone home-screen installation requires separate HTTPS hosting of the customer files. The public sample demo, if provided, is not a private buyer account.

This app does not connect to banks, send invoices, transfer payroll, calculate tax filings or replace a qualified accountant. For help contact HarborNestDigital via your Etsy order.
